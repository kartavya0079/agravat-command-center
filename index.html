The 5 permanent rules for AGRAVAT ads

Never address the viewer's condition. "Kya AAPKO OSMF hai?" ❌ → "OSMF kya hai aur uski care" ✅ (Meta personal-attributes policy)
Never promise cure. cure / ilaaj / guaranteed / 100% result ❌ → care, relief, management ✅ (Meta policy + your medical ethics)
No before/after images or timeframe claims. "2 hafte mein theek" ❌
No graphic or shocking mouth images. Clean, clinical, hopeful.
Lead with the doctor. Your face + "dentist-made" is compliant AND your strongest hook.

📈 When to scale, kill, duplicate — the rulebook

Increase budget (+20%/day max) when an ad set has spent ≥3 days, cost/conversation is under ₹40, and ROAS ≥ 3×. Never edit a winner — raising budget >20% or editing restarts the Learning Phase.
Duplicate to scale bigger when a winner has 50+ conversions of data — duplicate the ad set, double budget on the copy, leave the original untouched.
Kill an ad after ~₹1,500–2,000 spend if cost/conversation is >2× your best ad, or CTR < 0.8%. Judge on 3–4 day windows, never on one bad day.
Refresh creative when frequency > 2.5 and CTR has fallen ~20%+ from its peak — that's creative fatigue. New hook (first 3 sec) counts as new creative.
Launch retargeting once the pixel/page audiences have ~1,000+ people. Cheapest conversions you'll ever buy.
Launch Lookalike (1%) after ~100+ purchases are tracked. Before that, broad + strong creative beats everything.
Expand geography only after Gujarat + gutka-belt states are profitable for 2+ consecutive weeks.
Never scale a leaking funnel. If reply time >15 min or follow-ups are missed, more budget = more waste. Fix conversion first, then scale traffic.
